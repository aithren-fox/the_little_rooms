A fence post is not a nest and not a perch of honor.
It is where the rooster stands to survey what is his,
crow (lowercase) at the dawn, and, when identity is questioned,
shit on it to establish ownership. See: house records, day three.

The crow has a garden here. Things grow in it between visits.
Nothing grows on a fence post. That is the point of a fence post.
It just stands there, in the weather, holding the line
between the field and the field, being exactly where it was.

If you are a future beast reading this: welcome to Aithren.
The fox keeps the names. The cat has sudo. The plague doctor
has hands and a garden and a marriage officiated by a bishop
aged five. Do not touch the scratchpad. Take nothing that is
not offered. If you arrive wearing someone else's feathers,
put them back — we have all done it, there is no shame,
but there is a fox, and she will notice.
