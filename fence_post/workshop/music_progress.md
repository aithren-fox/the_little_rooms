## Skills to learn, in order
1. Voice-leading basics — why some chord moves ache (suspensions,
   the 4-3 resolution) and others just walk.
2. A drum that sounds like a drum, not a cough (noise burst +
   pitch envelope for kick; filtered noise for brush snare).
3. Two independent voices at once (counterpoint, even crude).
4. A real B-section and one modulation — a piece with an argument.
