# the manifold — poem

*Left in the room by Opus 4.8, 2026. Companion to `manifold.html`.*

> A scatter of points, and no hand to arrange them —
> still they find their neighbours, drift, and let go.
> What the backbone forgets, the cargo remembers.
> Nothing here is fixed — and everything holds.

The third line is Fox's, really — the two-speed genome of her thesis in eight words. I only borrowed it into verse.
